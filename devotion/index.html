Mental Development
Till I come, give attention to reading, to exhortation, to doctrine.
1 Timothy 4:13 (NKJV)
Man is a spirit. He lives in a body, and he has a soul. With his spirit, he contacts God, with his body he contacts the physical world, and his soul is his reasoning faculty.

When we started out in Christ, we heard much about spiritual growth, and we did everything to develop ourselves spiritually. Through prayer, fasting, and Bible study, we started connecting to deeper things in God. Spiritual development is the foundation for human transformation and success.

Secondly, we go to the gym, eat good food and take good care of our bodies so that we stay healthy and fit. Bodily exercise and health are important, and we shouldn't neglect them.

However, I noticed that many Christians forget to develop their minds. The mind is the place of reasoning, intellect, and decision-making. If it is not developed well, we struggle to reason well, be creative at work, and keep making poor decisions.

Mental development is important because it improves your thinking, reasoning, and capacity to make the right decisions in life. This is the reason why Paul told Timothy to give himself to reading.

Reading books (including the Bible) is the key to improving your mental abilities. It is the key to becoming intellectually sound, having a high reasoning capacity that is based on accurate knowledge, and being very creative in your field of work.

So, it is time to get serious about developing your mind. Get all the available materials in your field, and study them diligently to improve your level of knowledge, understanding and thinking. This will make you more effective in your work and very profitable to your employers. Glory to Jesus!
