Dwell on the Good
"And God created great whales, and every living creature that moveth, which the waters brought forth abundantly, after their kind, and every winged fowl after his kind: and God saw that it was good. And God made the beast of the earth after his kind, and cattle after their kind, and every thing that creepeth upon the earth after his kind: and God saw that it was good. And God saw every thing that he had made, and, behold, it was very good. And the evening and the morning were the sixth day."
Genesis 1:21, 25, 31 (KJV)

Several verses in Genesis chapter one reveal that God saw that what He created was good. The purpose of these verses is to show us the mindset of God. He is never negative. He always sees the good and doesn't focus on the evil.

This is very instructive because we all face negative situations. However, it is not what happens to us that really affects us but how we see and respond to it. You can choose to stay positive in the face of too much negativity around you. The key is dwelling on the little good that you can see.

Genesis 1:2 shows that the earth was in great confusion and empty, but God saw that it could be changed and become good. He decided to start creating what He wanted and focused on the new that He created. He didn't think about what was destroyed in the past; He simply saw the good that He created to replace it.

How you see your life is important. Do you see good or evil? Do you see lack or abundance? What you focus on will control your feelings, beliefs, and actions. This will affect how you relate to God and people, how you perform at your workplace, and eventually affect your destiny.

Negative thinking is satanic. 2 Corinthians 10:4–6 strongly urges us to cast down evil imaginations and thoughts. We should always align our minds to what is good and true in every situation. When a bad situation happens to you, learn to respond to it from the standpoint of what the Word of God says. That is what will keep you aligned with what is good.

In Romans 12:21, God told us not to be overcome by evil but to overcome evil with good. He didn't say evil would not come — it will come. However, He showed us how to overcome it. Focusing on the evil around you doesn't take it away. Instead, it magnifies it and hinders the flow of God's power in your life. It is by focusing on what is good that you overcome evil.

Focusing on the good is part of spiritual warfare. It energises your faith, fills you with the power of God, and lifts you above the negative situation. Glory to Jesus!

✦ Prayer

I make Your Word my focus and source of thoughts to meditate upon. I cast down every negative thought and bring them into subjection to what Your Word says about me, in Jesus' Mighty Name!
