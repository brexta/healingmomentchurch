Empowered to Lead
"But you shall receive power (ability, efficiency, and might) when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you shall be My witnesses in Jerusalem and all Judea and Samaria and to the ends (the very bounds) of the earth."
Acts 1:8 (AMP)

Leadership is the ability to influence and impact lives positively through the accomplishment of a God-given vision and assignment.

Because leadership is about people, it is impossible to carry out effectively in your own strength. Our opening scripture reveals that it is through the Holy Spirit that we receive the ability and efficiency and might to become great leaders. The Holy Spirit is the key to becoming an effective and efficient leader.

Isaiah 11:2 reveals the several abilities the Holy Spirit imparts to you as a leader to make you able and effective. He imparts to you wisdom, knowledge, understanding, counsel, might, and the fear of the Lord. He also makes you of quick understanding and gives you the ability to accurately discern and judge between right and wrong.

Before David became King, God anointed him, "and the Spirit of the Lord came upon David from that day forward" (1 Samuel 16:13). He couldn't become King without the empowerment of the Holy Spirit.

In Acts 6, there was a food conflict, and the Apostles said, "choose for yourselves men of honest report full of the Holy Spirit and wisdom whom we can appoint over this business" (Acts 6:3). Scripture says, "…and they chose Stephen a man full of faith and of the Holy Spirit…." (v.5). Hallelujah!

These scriptures attest to the truth that even the lowest level of leadership cannot effectively be filled and carried out in human strength and wisdom. It takes the wisdom of God and the power of the Holy Spirit to become a leader and lead effectively at every level.

So, as a worship leader, pastor, mother, father, or employer, seek God for the empowerment of the Holy Spirit to help you become an effective leader. He will empower you with all the strength and wisdom you need. Glory to Jesus!
