Leadership Series: Believe in People
"Moreover you shall select from all the people able men, such as fear God, men of truth, hating covetousness; and place such over them to be rulers of thousands, rulers of hundreds, rulers of fifties, and rulers of tens. And let them judge the people at all times. Then it will be that every great matter they shall bring to you, but every small matter they themselves shall judge. So it will be easier for you, for they will bear the burden with you."
Exodus 18:21–22 (NKJV)

Leadership is about impacting and influencing people's lives positively. The purpose and goal of a true leader is to help people change and become what God ordained them to be in this world.

True leaders seek to awaken, stir, and nurture the potential within every follower so that they become fruitful and profitable in their God-given assignment. They are helpers positioned by God in our lives, to help as many as they can rise to their appointed place of leadership.

It is therefore impossible to be a great leader if you don't believe in people. True leaders believe in people. That is what causes them to invest heavily in their growth and success. It is what makes them patient with the weak and love the unloved until they change and become great leaders in God's kingdom.

One of the proofs of believing in people is delegation. Delegation means allowing others to handle key tasks, as part of their training into leadership. This is the wisdom Jethro passed on to Moses. He advised him to stop doing all the work alone, and appoint able men to help him do the smaller tasks.

Delegating tasks to others as a leader helps them know you believe in them. It assures them that they matter. It is one of the ways you encourage them to know that God has a purpose and plan for their life. By delegating crucial tasks to them, you help them practically discover that they have the potential to also become leaders.

So, in your leadership, encourage delegation as a way of telling people, "I believe in you." Glory to Jesus.
