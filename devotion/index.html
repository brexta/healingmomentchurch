The Fear of Missing It
"He who had received one talent also came forward, saying, Master, I knew you to be a harsh and hard man, reaping where you did not sow, and gathering where you had not winnowed [the grain]. So I was afraid, and I went and hid your talent in the ground. Here you have what is your own."
Matthew 25:24–25 (AMP)

One of the greatest traps of leadership potential is fear, especially the fear of missing it or missing God. This is what crippled the man with one talent.

As children of God, we really love God and fear His Name. So, we are always seeking to do what pleases Him and agrees with His perfect will for our lives. This is a good attitude, and we should keep it up.

However, Satan also uses this same attitude against us many times. He does this by planting thoughts that question our decisions and actions in and for God. By planting the fear of "missing it," he seeks to make us bury our talents in the sand and not fulfill our God-given destiny. His ultimate aim is to actually draw us away from acting on what we know to be true.

Child of God, understand that faith is about trusting God and His Word and not your mind. Your mind may have doubts, but so long as you know that what you are doing is in agreement with God's Word, don't fear to step out and do it.

Secondly, you don't have to be perfect to please God; you simply have to be bold enough to believe. Faith is what pleases God, not your perfect works (Heb. 11:6). God honors faith and not perfection. He looks on the heart and not on outside things. Trust Him to help you know when you are off track and bring you back again. Trust Him to protect you from missing it, and don't try to do it in your own strength.

Test everything by the Word. It is your sure guide for finding the peace and confidence you need to act without missing it. Also, know that it is better to try and miss it than to sit back and never do anything. God will help you when you try, but He can't help you if you do nothing at all. Glory to Jesus!

✦ Prayer

I believe every word You have spoken to me, and I step out in faith to act on each of them. As I do so, Your power will back me up, and Your hand will be upon me to cause me to succeed in all I do, in Jesus' Mighty Name!
