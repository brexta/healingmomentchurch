Direction and Speed
"Where there is no vision, the people perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he."
Proverbs 29:18 (KJV)

Direction refers to knowing the way to take in the course of life. It is God's will for you to have a clear direction for your life. It is the key to defeating distractions, stagnation, and continuous progress.

The key to having a clear direction for your life is receiving divine vision from God. Vision reveals to you God's plan and purpose for your life. This helps you know which activities to invest in and which ones to avoid. It also helps you know what your best skills and gifts are and what skills you don't have.

The purpose of divine vision is to help us live a clearly guided life in this world so that we don't waste our time and energy on things that don't matter. It is the key to knowing what to do and how to do it effectively.

Scripture says, "Where there is no vision, the people perish." Divine direction from God keeps your life from entering into destructive paths that can frustrate your destiny. It keeps you focused and firmly establishes your feet in the path God ordained for you to walk in.

Ecclesiastes 10:15 says, "The labor of fools wearies them, for they do not even know how to go to the city!" Stagnation and frustration easily come to anyone who lacks clear direction for their life. A clear direction for your life is the foundation for accuracy, effectiveness, and efficiency in all things.

The ultimate goal of divine direction is to help you run faster. By having clear details about your life purpose and mission, it becomes easy for you to lay aside every irrelevant thing and run your race with precision. So, it is wisdom to seek God for a clear direction for your life, family, business, and ministry. Divine direction determines divine speed. Glory to Jesus!
