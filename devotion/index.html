Healed So That You Serve God

Apostle Julius P. Isabirye

"So He touched her hand, and the fever left her. Then she got up and began to serve Him."

Matthew 8:15 (HCSB)

A young girl was brought to a preacher for healing. The preacher asked her why she wanted to be healed, and she answered, "I am a ballet dancer, and I want God to heal me so that I can return to dancing again."

God is merciful and loving. He is concerned about all our needs, and that is why He wants our bodies healed. Divine healing is an expression of His love and kindness towards us. God heals us because He is touched by our pains and sorrows.

However, we should note that God's greater purpose is for us to use our strength to serve Him. God heals us so that we are healthy and strong enough to carry out His will and purpose in this world. We are meant to use our strength to serve Him.

Peter's mother-in-law demonstrated this wisdom, and God uses her example to show us the right pattern. Once she got healed, she immediately rose up and started serving the Lord. These are the kind of people whom God heals and never wants to see sick again — people who desire health not for selfish reasons but to be able to serve God's purposes effectively.

So, why do you want to be healed? Is your reason spiritual or carnal? Seek healing with God's purposes in mind. Use your health and strength for His service. Glory to Jesus!
