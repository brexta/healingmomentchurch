Opening Deaf Ears
"And in that day shall the deaf hear the words of the book, and the eyes of the blind shall see out of obscurity and out of darkness."
Isaiah 29:18 (KJV)

Every miracle in the Bible contains an instruction and a message. Jesus went about doing good and healing all who were oppressed of the devil with a purpose in mind. His purpose was to instruct and teach us in the ways of God.

He raised the dead to reveal that He is the resurrection and the Life (John 11:25). He forgave sins, to show us that He is the Lamb of God that takes away the sins of the whole world.

Why did He open deaf ears? First, we need to understand that hearing is essential in our walk of faith. Romans 10:17 says, "Faith comes by hearing and hearing by the Word of God." So, hearing connects you to faith in the word of God.

Secondly, Jesus said, "My sheep hear my voice and the voice of the stranger will they not follow" (John 10:4–5, 27). So, hearing connects you to the voice of Jesus, and this protects you from following the voice of the enemy.

From these two examples, we understand that God's purpose for opening deaf ears wasn't simply about performing a miracle. He was helping the once deaf ears start hearing what brings faith. He was also helping those once disconnected from God's voice and confused by the devil, connect to the true voice that comes from God.

So, deaf ears are not only physical but represent people who are disconnected from God because they are disconnected from His voice and His words. They doubt God because their ears haven't connected to the hearing of faith. They are still held captive to the voice of the stranger (Satan) because Jesus is not yet their shepherd.

Hearing ears represent people who quickly connect to God's word and believe it. They are the people who have come to know Jesus as their shepherd and continually follow His voice wherever He leads them.

Salvation is the master key to opening deaf ears. Once you become part of God's sheep, your ears are open to hear and understand His word and His voice. Secondly, God opens deaf ears through the teaching ministry. As you hear the word of God taught, your ears are opened to hear God's voice behind the spoken Word. These are the two ways God opens deaf ears, in Jesus' Name!

📖 Bible Reading Isaiah 50–52 · 1 Thessalonians 5
🙏 Meditation Isaiah 29:18
✦ Prayer

Heavenly Father, I thank You for this truth. I yield myself to the Word of God as it is taught, that my ears may continually be opened to receive light, knowledge, and understanding for my next level. I resist every form of deafness and declare that I have hearing ears, in Jesus' Mighty Name!
