What Can You Do?
"For the kingdom of heaven is like a man traveling to a far country, who called his own servants and delivered his goods to them. And to one he gave five talents, to another two, and to another one, to each according to his own ability… Then he who had received the five talents went and traded with them, and made another five talents. And likewise he who had received two gained two more also. But he who had received one went and dug in the ground, and hid his lord's money."
Matthew 25:14–18 (NKJV)

The feeling of inability is one of the main causes of failure in life. To succeed, you must believe that you are able. At least, though you can't do all things, know that there are things you can do.

God has not left anyone without any divinely given ability. We all possess treasures untapped and skills unused, which if we discover and put to use, they will both change our lives and the lives of many others.

What can you do? Have you ever sat down to ask yourself this question? Do you really know the potentials within you? Have you ever taken an inventory of your life?

Success comes when you discover what you can do and channel all your efforts towards doing it. Failure, on the other hand, is a result of not knowing what you can do, or doubting your abilities and failing to act on them. Failure is rooted in one's concentration on their inabilities instead of focusing on their abilities.

As you look at your weaknesses and focus on them, they are magnified, and they swallow up your strengths. It is wisdom to discover your strengths, greatest skills and abilities, and focus on them. Magnify your strengths above your weaknesses. Develop your greatest abilities, and they will put you over and above the limitations of your weaknesses.

The man with one talent failed to put it to use because he focused on his inability, and it blinded him from the ability his master saw in him. Your inabilities don't define you; it is your abilities that truly define you and determine your success.

Understand that what you can do is more important than what you cannot do. Your strengths and God-given abilities are more important than your weaknesses and inabilities. So, start today to channel your thoughts, resources, and time towards building your abilities. Make them your focus, and they will bring you great rewards and fulfillment in life, in Jesus' Name.
