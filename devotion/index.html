Leadership Series: The Power Of Vision
Put away from thee a froward mouth, and perverse lips put far from thee. Let thine eyes look right on, and let thine eyelids look straight before thee. Ponder the path of thy feet, and let all thy ways be established. Turn not to the right hand nor to the left: remove thy foot from evil.
Proverbs 4:24-27 (KJV)
One of my mentors said, "You were not born to do everything." That statement shows that though there are many activities around you, you are not called to participate in all of them. It shows that you were born for a specific purpose.

Even in ministry, though we all preach from the Bible, we are called to preach and teach specific parts of the Bible. Every pastor is given a specific message to the world. That is the reason why God keeps calling and anointing more pastors every day.

Divine vision is the mother of focus. It is the key to looking straight ahead without turning to the right hand or the left. It is the key to avoiding distractions.

Divine vision also eliminates competition, which is one of the greatest reasons for having 'the excess' in your life. For example, Moses was born to be a deliverer, Nehemiah was born to be a builder and repairer of the broken wall of Jerusalem, and Esther was born to be Queen. None of them tried to do what the other was born for because that would be a distraction to their specific destiny.

Having a clear vision for your life is the number one platform for eliminating 'the excess' from your life. The excess refers to the things that are unnecessary and not profitable to your life and destiny.

Divine vision comes to trim your life and make you focused only on what is important in fulfilling God's purpose for your life. It is the main source of disciplined behaviour and action. That is why Proverbs 29:18 says, "Where there is no vision, the people run wild." Indiscipline and lack of focus are easily eliminated when divine vision is discovered and pursued.

So, what is God's vision for your life? Find it out, and it will help you become focused, empower you to remove the excess from your life, and make you a great leader who positively influences many lives for God, in Jesus Name!
